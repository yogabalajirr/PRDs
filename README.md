## How each PRD is structured

Every document follows the same backbone, so they're quick to read and compare:

- **Overview / TL;DR** — what it is and who it's for, in a few lines
- **Problem statement & background** — the user problem and why it matters now
- **Market & competitive landscape** — where the product fits and how it differs
- **Goals, non-goals & success metrics** — outcomes I'm aiming for, and what I'm deliberately *not* doing
- **Personas & user stories** — who uses it and what they're trying to do
- **Requirements** — prioritized (P0/P1/P2) with acceptance criteria
- **Non-functional requirements** — security, reliability, performance
- **Release plan** — how the work is phased
- **Risks, assumptions & dependencies**
- **Open questions** — what's still unresolved, and who owns each answer
